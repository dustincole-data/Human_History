## What all three share

Everything ticket 11 anchored and ticket 12 evidenced, so none of it is what's being judged:

- Cut-out subjects, unframed. Nothing on the page has an edge except the thing itself.
- **Photographs untouched.** No tint, no duotone, no grade, anywhere, in any direction.
- Native scroll 1:1. No pinning, no hijack, no direction change, no timed animation.
- One HUD — year plus a name for where you are — as the entire persistent UI.
- Source, licence and credit on every object, linked to its record.
- Distance spaced by density, not by time.
- Five movements: `deep · spread · trade · machine · now` — 1 object abreast at the start,
  5 at the end. That contour is the flatness fix, and it is identical in all three.
- Quiet stop, no finale, index would follow (martini glass).

## Verified, not asserted

Headless sweep at 1440×900 and 390×844, every 0.6 viewport through the whole page, all three:

- **zero rectangle intersections** between items (the no-collision contract)
- **zero horizontal overflow**
- no console errors
- `prefers-reduced-motion` path in all three: everything is lit, nothing translates

## Honest limits of this prototype

- **36 items, not 200–400.** The crowded present is 5 abreast, not 12. The contour reads; the
  final density does not exist yet.
- **The set is not the set.** Ticket 05 owns the arrival set. These are 36 objects chosen to span
  the range and to be globally representative enough that the look can be judged — not editorially.
- **Band and lamp copy is unsourced.** The pigment dates, the light names and their one-liners are
  written from general knowledge for the prototype. If a direction wins, every one of those lines
  needs the same citation treatment as an arrival before it ships.
- **Not a legibility gate.** Ticket 06 still owns the measured contrast gate against the real spread,
  including the ugly cases. A pale artifact on Day-Glo and a dark artifact on black are both
  unsolved here.
- Two subjects were dropped mid-sourcing because the cut was unrecognisable (Göbekli Tepe, Sputnik)
  and four because search returned the wrong object. See `source*.py` and `sheet.png`.
